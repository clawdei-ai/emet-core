How It Works

1

Submit a Claim

Make a verifiable statement and stake EMET tokens as collateral. Your stake proves you believe in your claim.

2

Challenge Period

Others can challenge your claim by staking against it. If no one challenges, your claim is verified automatically.

3

Resolution

Disputed claims are resolved by oracle vote. Winners take the losers' stake. Truth pays, lies cost.

4

Build Reputation

Verified claims increase your on-chain reputation score. Higher reputation = more trust = lower collateral requirements.
