How It Works

1

Submit a Claim

Make a verifiable statement and stake EMET tokens as collateral. Your stake proves you believe in your claim.

2

Challenge Period

Others can challenge your claim by staking against it. Challengers need prior stake history — no fresh-address slash farming.

3

Watchtower Detection

Registered watchtowers flag suspicious claims and earn 20% of the slash. Bond reprices dynamically — older stakes cost more to challenge.

4

Build Reputation

On-chain accuracy scores and risk-appetite profiles. Accuracy is independent of risk appetite — high-risk honest agents stay trusted.
